🎱 PinBounce'55

An arcade game inspired by iconic pinball, with a twist.

Build stations, unlock power-ups, and skilfully juggle the ball to defend your line from incoming enemies. The best score wins!

🎮 How to Play
Key	Action
A	Trigger the left paddle
D	Trigger the right paddle
Earn points by defeating incoming enemies, either by hitting them with the ball or with special stations.
Spend your score to unlock power-ups or construct buildings that help you progress further.
👾 Enemies
Enemy	Colour	HP	Notes	Points
Classic	🟠 Orange	1	The basic enemy	X
Heavy	⚫ Black	3	Tough and slow to take down	X
Ghost	🟣 Purple	2	Can phase through some buildings	X
⚡ Power-Ups

Once purchased, a power-up can be used infinitely with no additional cost.

Power-Up	Effect
❄️ Freeze	Freezes all enemies for 5 seconds
🛡️ Shield	Blocks all incoming damage for 5 seconds
💥 Shrapnel	Explodes, releasing shrapnel in all directions
🎱 Double Ball	Spawns a second ball for 10 seconds
🏗️ Buildings

You can build structures to help defend your line.

⚠️ All buildings have limited durability and will eventually break.

How to Build
Press the [+] button next to the building you want to construct.
Move the building anywhere within the building zone on the table.
Use [Scroll] to rotate it.
Press [LMB] to confirm placement, or [RMB] to cancel.
Available Buildings
Building	Description
🧱 Wall	A simple defensive wall that blocks enemies
🌀 Spinner	A spinning mechanism that kicks enemies and your ball
🔴 Laser	Periodically activates and burns enemies
🔵 Bumper	Launches nearby objects and bodies away
➡️ Boost Pad	Launches objects in a predefined direction

Good luck, and keep the ball in play! 🎯
